# Initial decisions

1. BARON is a separate protected management application.
2. Two directors receive separate accounts with equivalent day-to-day permissions.
3. A director must not be able to deactivate, demote, or delete the other director through ordinary application actions.
4. Future specialist accounts use a separate role; no specialist account is required in the first release.
5. Commission is configured per deal as either a fixed amount or a percentage.
6. The audit log records important actions. Application-level append-only enforcement and privileged access controls remain to be implemented.
7. Equal system permissions do not by themselves define how company profit is distributed.
